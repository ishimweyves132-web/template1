# Notices

## Dictionary data (Android APK build)
The full dictionary bundled in the Android app is generated at build time from **Wiktionary** (English edition), obtained through
**kaikki.org**, which publishes machine-readable extracts made with **Wiktextract** (by Tatu Ylönen).
Wiktionary text is available under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0** licence and the **GNU Free Documentation Licence**.
The generated dictionary pack is therefore distributed under those same licences, with attribution to Wiktionary contributors.
The licence applies to the dictionary data, not to this app's code.
Details and per-entry history: https://en.wiktionary.org · https://kaikki.org

## Built-in starter words and phrasebook
`src/data/words.ts` and `src/data/phrases.ts` are original to this project.
